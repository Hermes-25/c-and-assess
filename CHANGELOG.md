# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-03

First documented public source release.

### Candidate

- Google sign-in, assessment discovery and registration.
- Timed test interface with palette, review flags and batched recovery saves.
- Objective scoring, receipt and released analysis.
- Rank, percentile, accuracy, time, topic/difficulty and question-wise review.
- Across-mock trends, leaderboard, solutions and private error labels.

### Organizer

- Assessment builder and lifecycle controls.
- CSV and image-ZIP validation, preview and safe paper publishing.
- Registration and participant views.
- Batch result generation, exclusions, re-evaluation and separate score/solution release.
- Audit history and operations guidance.

### Engineering

- Next.js 16, React 19, Vinext and Cloudflare-compatible runtime.
- Drizzle/D1 migrations and object-storage bindings.
- Same-origin mutation checks, security headers and role enforcement.
- Scoring, schema, load-rehearsal and CI checks.

Known limitations and upgrade paths are tracked in docs/LIMITATIONS_AND_ROADMAP.md.
