# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Students taking placement mocks, internship mocks and other club assessments, and C&A organizers running them for cohorts that may grow to 2,000–4,000 candidates. Candidates may use average laptops, phones and uneven connections; organizers need a workflow that does not require engineering knowledge.

## Product Purpose

C&Assess lets organizers publish structured assessments from spreadsheets, run a fair timed test at campus scale, and give each candidate practical post-test analysis. Success means a reliable test day, simple operations, useful feedback, and no mandatory per-candidate platform fee.

## Brand Personality

Confident, rigorous, generous. The product should feel like a focused C&A learning initiative: serious enough for a placement round, calm enough for an anxious candidate, and clearly educational rather than corporate-surveillance software.

## Anti-references

Do not copy Unstop's brand, visual chrome, or proprietary interface. Avoid noisy enterprise dashboards, excessive cards, gamified test visuals, webcam-surveillance language, and decorative SaaS gradients.

## Design Principles

1. The test is the task: remove distractions once an attempt starts.
2. Explain every rule before enforcing it.
3. Save quietly and recover gracefully on weak connections.
4. Turn scores into next actions, not vanity charts.
5. Keep organizer workflows spreadsheet-friendly and reversible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Every action must work by keyboard, focus must remain visible, status cannot rely on color alone, motion respects reduced-motion preferences, and layouts must remain usable on narrow screens.
